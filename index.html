gutter-desktop: 1.5rem margin: 1rem margin-desktop: 2rem space-xs: 0.25rem space-sm: 0.5rem space-md: 1rem space-lg: 1.5rem space-xl: 2.5rem --- ## Brand & Style This design system translates the clinical, militaristic telemetry of a starship cockpit into a galactic health and biometric diagnostic station. Designed for pilots, troopers, and fleet commanders, the interface communicates vital diagnostic data with cold precision, tactical clarity, and absolute authority. The aesthetic fuses **Utilitarian Military Sci-Fi** with **Tactical Holographic Glassmorphism**: - **Tactical Cockpit HUD:** High-density readouts, status quadrants, monospaced diagnostic telemetry, and strict grid alignments mirror advanced avionics consoles. - **Translucent Holographic Substrates:** Panels exist as physical projected glass within the cockpit, utilizing deep cosmic navy bases with delicate cyan luminescent boundaries. - **Diagnostic Triage Colorway:** Functional illumination isolates bio-metrics instantly—cyan denotes operational stability, amber/gold denotes caution and command thresholds, while deep space voids ensure high optical contrast under emergency conditions. ## Colors The palette is engineered strictly for darkened cockpit environments, ensuring zero visual fatigue while elevating high-priority biometric telemetry. ### Primary Role: Holo-Cyan (`#00AEEF`) The primary system light source. Used for active diagnostics, biometric waveforms, scan lines, key calls-to-action, active radio/checkbox states, and luminous focal points. ### Secondary Role: Imperial Gold / Tactical Amber (`#D4AF37`) A command-tier accent indicating physiological alerts, threshold boundaries, biometric warnings (e.g., BMI risk factors, elevated heart rate), and authenticated clearance tiers. ### Structural Neutral & Substrates - **Deep Space Black (`#080B14`):** Canvas backdrop representing unlit cockpit space. - **Cosmic Navy (`#0D1322`):** Base layer for telemetry containers, side-docks, and biometric card surfaces. - **Translucent Holo Surface:** `rgba(13, 19, 34, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. - **Holo-Cyan Border Stroke:** `1px solid rgba(0, 174, 239, 0.30)`. - **High-Alert Crimson (`#FF3B30`):** Reserved exclusively for critical biological failure, extreme BMI divergence, and terminal warnings. - **Subdued Phosphor Neutral (`#8A99AD`):** Used for non-active coordinate readouts, telemetry timestamps, and secondary metric labels. ## Typography The typographic hierarchy enforces tactical discipline. Display headings, metric readouts, and status codes leverage **Space Grotesk** for its technical, angular proportions and military avionics rhythm. Body analysis, clinical notes, and dense physiological diagnoses rely on **Inter** to ensure micro-legibility at varying viewing distances. ### Typographic Rules: - **Telemetry Encodings & Labels:** All telemetry tags, metric parameters (e.g., `BMI_INDEX`, `HEART_RATE_BPM`, `O2_SAT`), and section anchors must render in uppercase with expanded tracking (`0.12em` to `0.15em`). - **Telemetry Readouts:** Numerical outputs utilize tabular figures for dynamic metric stability during active scanning. ## Layout & Spacing The interface functions on a rigid, high-density modular 12-column grid resembling an active flight console. ### Structural Rhythm - **Modular Cockpit Quadrants:** Telemetry layouts must arrange into structured cockpit panels (Left Dock: Diagnostic History; Center: Vital Hologram/BMI Matrix; Right Dock: Sensor Stream). - **Responsive Adaptations:** - **Desktop (1024px+):** Full 12-column telemetry array with persistent status rails and biometric radar grids. - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout; left and right diagnostic docks reflow into stacked collapsible drawer consoles. - **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column single-stream telemetry stack; priority order locks Center Bio-Gauge first, followed by tactical metric inputs and health summary drawers. ## Elevation & Depth Spatial separation relies on holographic layering, luminescence, and glass refraction rather than traditional diffuse drop-shadows. ### The Holo-Stack Hierarchy: 1. **Base Cockpit Canvas (Void):** `#080B14` flat background with faint tactical 32px square grid lines rendered in `rgba(0, 174, 239, 0.04)`. 2. **Standard Holo-Panel (Level 1):** Background `rgba(13, 19, 34, 0.65)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and perimeter border `1px solid rgba(0, 174, 239, 0.25)`. 3. **Elevated Sensor Pod (Level 2):** Background `rgba(13, 19, 34, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`, border `1px solid rgba(0, 174, 239, 0.50)`, and an inner ambient glow: `box-shadow: inset 0 0 16px rgba(0, 174, 239, 0.12)`. 4. **Critical Alert / Active Targeting (Overlay):** Background `rgba(13, 19, 34, 0.95)`, border `1px solid #D4AF37`, and external beam glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`. ## Shapes The physical silhouette is industrial, angular, and functional. Elements use soft `0.25rem` corners to prevent optical glare while maintaining an armored, precision-machined edge. - **Chamfered Cockpit Accents:** High-level biometric cards feature 45-degree clipped corners (chamfers) achieved via clipping paths (`clip-path: polygon(0 0, calc(100% - 10px) 0, 100% 10px, 100% 100%, 0 100%)`) to enhance the starship instrumentation appearance. - **Sensor Grids & Dividers:** Separators are razor-thin `1px` lines accented with intermittent tick marks or crosshair motifs at intersections. ## Components ### Telemetry Buttons - **Primary (Execute Scan / Commit Analysis):** Background `rgba(0, 174, 239, 0.15)`, border `1px solid #00AEEF`, text `#00AEEF`, uppercase `Space Grotesk` font. Active hover triggers solid `#00AEEF` background with `#080B14` text and `0 0 16px rgba(0, 174, 239, 0.60)` external bloom. - **Command / Alert Tier:** Background `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`, border `1px solid #D4AF37`, text `#D4AF37`. Hover yields amber bloom `0 0 16px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.50)`. - **Secondary (Calibrate / Abort):** Ghost button with border `1px solid rgba(138, 153, 173, 0.30)`, text `#8A99AD`. ### Telemetry Status Indicators & Chips - Miniature rectangular badges with fixed height (`22px`), uppercase `label-caps` typography, and leading pulsing status pip: - **Optimal (Normal BMI):** Cyan border, cyan text, breathing dot (`#00AEEF`). - **Warning (Over/Under Mass):** Amber border, amber text, steady dot (`#D4AF37`). - **Critical (Hazardous Bio-Variance):** Crimson border, crimson text, rapid flashing dot (`#FF3B30`). ### Input Fields & Biometric Sliders - **Numeric Fields (Height, Weight, Age):** Deep cosmic navy container (`rgba(13, 19, 34, 0.90)`), bottom border `2px solid rgba(0, 174, 239, 0.40)`. Focused state illuminates the bottom border to `#00AEEF` with a soft blue horizontal underglow. Placeholder text in `#8A99AD`. Unit tags (e.g., `KG`, `CM`, `STD_ROTATION`) pinned to the right edge in uppercase `label-caps`. - **Telemetry Sliders:** Sleek track in `rgba(0, 174, 239, 0.20)`, dynamic fill in `#00AEEF`, and a hexagonal or angular slider thumb with intense cyan core glow. ### Checkboxes & Radio Toggles - Custom square components (`16px`) with 1px soft corners. Unchecked: `1px solid rgba(0, 174, 239, 0.40)` over transparent background. Checked: `#00AEEF` fill with cockpit crosshair or inverted tick icon, casting a localized `0 0 8px rgba(0, 174, 239, 0.50)` halo. ### Holographic Telemetry Cards - Encapsulated biometric readouts (e.g., BMI Gauge Card, Body Composition Radar, Caloric Output Matrix). - Panels feature a top tactical bracket: a 2px horizontal accent line spanning the top-left edge with a corner-anchored coordinates label (e.g., `SEC_04 // BIO_MONITOR`). - Ambient back-glow on hover reinforces depth and holographic integrity.
